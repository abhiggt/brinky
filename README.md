## Why Brinky exists

**Our eyes weren't made for screens.** They were made for horizons. For faces across a table, for birds overhead, for the slow change of light over a day. Not for a bright rectangle held at arm's length, ten hours at a time.

**Our bodies weren't made to sit this much.** They were made to wander, to reach, to turn around and fetch water. Instead they fold toward a desk and forget to drink, until four in the afternoon, when the words start to swim.

None of this is a lack of willpower. Screens are made to hold your attention, and they are very good at it. Your screen will never tell you to look away. It will never tell you to drink, or stand, or blink.

**So we made a small friend who will.** It notices when you've been staring too long and asks, gently, at a good moment: blink. Look far away. Have a glass. Stretch a little. It doesn't nag, and it doesn't watch. It simply remembers, so you don't have to.

*Be gentle with your eyes. They have a lifetime of sunsets to see.*

## Three small habits, kept for you

### 01 · Blink
**Your cursor blinks first.** Every twenty minutes your cursor blinks a few times, so a break never arrives out of nowhere. Then Brinky takes twenty seconds with you: look far away, or follow one gentle exercise with just your eyes. In between, a soft blink nudge reminds you to blink along.

### 02 · Drink
**Water, without the counting.** Brinky spreads your glasses across the day and always tells you where you are: which glass, and how much. Pick how it asks:

- **A wave** washes up the page like the sea coming up a beach, lingers, then slowly slides back. Brinky floats in it.
- **A little glass** lands right next to your cursor.
- **A note** waits quietly in the corner.

Not now? One tap and it asks again in 7 minutes. Winter, or drinking less? Turn water reminders off with one switch; the glasses you log are still counted.

### 03 · Move
**Your cursor moves, so you do.** Every forty-five minutes your cursor gets up for a few seconds: a long stretch, a brisk little walk, or a quick kickabout, with a small note beside it. Nothing pops up and nothing needs closing.

## It waits for the right moment

A reminder in the middle of a thought is just one more interruption. So Brinky taps you gently first, and quietly steps back whenever now isn't the time.

- **While you type,** it waits for a pause.
- **On calls** in Meet, Zoom or Teams, it stays silent.
- **In fullscreen** or a big video, it holds off.
- **When you step away,** that time counts as rest.

## One click away

Brinky lives in your toolbar. Open it to see when your next eye break is, log a glass, rest your eyes right now, or pause everything for a meeting. When the last glass of the day is in, Brinky notices.

## How to use: five steps, one minute

Brinky isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it as a folder. It's the same Brinky, and it runs entirely in your browser.

1. **Download and unzip.** [Download Brinky.zip](https://abhiggt.github.io/brinky/download/Brinky.zip) and double-click it to get a folder called Brinky. Keep it somewhere it can stay, like Documents.
2. **Open your extensions.** Paste `chrome://extensions` into the address bar. On Edge it's `edge://extensions`, on Brave `brave://extensions`.
3. **Turn on Developer mode.** The switch in the top-right corner. It only lets you add extensions from a folder; nothing else changes.
4. **Load unpacked.** Press the new button and choose the Brinky folder. Brinky switches on and says hello.
5. **Pin Brinky.** Open the puzzle-piece menu and press the pin next to Brinky, so the drop sits in your toolbar.

Updating later? Unzip the new Brinky over the old folder, then press the reload arrow on its card.

## Questions, answered

**Is Brinky really free?**
Yes. No account, no ads and no paid tier.

**Will it interrupt my work?**
It tries hard not to. A break always gets a heads-up, waits while you type, and stays away from calls and fullscreen video. One press skips or snoozes it.

**Will it slow my browser down?**
No. Brinky only appears in the tab you're looking at, at the moment it's needed, and it lightens itself on slower computers.

**Why does it ask to read and change every site?**
To draw a break or a glass of water on the page you're on, and to notice a call so it can stay quiet. It never reads what's on the page.

**What happens to my data?**
Your settings and your daily counts stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**How do I remove it?**
On the Extensions page, press Remove on Brinky's card, then delete the folder. Nothing is left behind.
