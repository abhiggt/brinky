# What's new in Brinky

## 8 November 2026: the big refresh

A new Brinky, rebuilt around three small habits: rest your eyes, drink water, move your body.

### A new look
- **Brinky has a live face.** One soft blue drop that blinks, follows your pointer, laughs when you tickle him, gets sleepy when you pause, and looks happy when your water goal is done.
- **One calm design everywhere:** paper and ink, one blue, and the Figtree typeface across the popup, the walkthrough, the settings and every reminder.

### Water
- **The wave is the sea coming up a beach.** One see-through sheet of water washes up the page with a lacy foam edge, lingers, then slowly slides back. Brinky floats in the shallows.
- **A soft whoosh** as it arrives and a quieter one as it leaves. No droplets, no rumble.
- **Light on any computer.** The wave lightens itself on slower machines, and shows a simple version where it can't be drawn, so a reminder always appears.
- **A little glass** now lands next to your cursor as a smooth drawn glass with a paper note, and drains when you drink.
- **A note** is a wider, calmer card with your glasses for the day.
- **"Later" means 7 minutes** by default. You can pick anything from 3 to 30 in settings.

### Eyes
- Your cursor blinks a few times before every eye break, so it never arrives out of nowhere.
- Pick how often blink nudges come, or turn them off, in one tap.
- After a couple of skipped breaks, the next one is just ten seconds.

### Move
- Every forty-five minutes your cursor gets up: a long stretch, a brisk little walk or a quick kickabout, with a small note beside it. Choose one or let Brinky mix them.

### The toolbar popup
- **Simpler and smaller.** It fits without scrolling, and long names never get cut off.
- **A ring counts down** to your next eye break, with "Rest now" beside it.
- **Little glasses fill with water** as you log them.
- **Pause** for 15 minutes, an hour or the rest of the day in one tap.

### Walkthrough and settings
- The walkthrough fits on a small laptop screen without scrolling.
- An evening recap shows how your day went.
- Right-click any page for a quick eye break, a glass or a pause.
- Settings now end with a small note: made by Abhijeet.
