Brinky · Blink & Drink

Hi, I'm Brinky!

I'm a little water drop who helps you blink and drink. I'll remind you to rest your eyes and to sip some water, and I'll stay out of the way when you're busy.

  • Short look-away breaks to stop end-of-day eye strain
  • A water wave now and then, with a daily goal
  • Quiet during calls, videos, screen sharing and mid-sentence

How should your breaks work?

You can switch any time with one tap in the toolbar popup.

Every longer break comes with a few gentle moves, and I'll show you how.

How much water today?

Most people do well with around 8 glasses. Your progress lives in the toolbar popup.
