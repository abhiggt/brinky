Brinky is a Chrome extension that looks after your eyes, your water and your posture while you work, and knows when to leave you alone.

Download Brinky

Free, with no account and no tracking. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

Not another 20-20-20 timer

The 20-20-20 rule is where Brinky starts, not where it stops. Pick the rhythm that fits your work, and Brinky fits eye breaks, water, movement and posture around it.

It knows when to wait

A reminder that lands mid-sentence gets dismissed, and soon they all do. So before it interrupts, Brinky checks what you're doing.

  • Mid-sentenceIf you're typing, the break waits for a pause, up to 30 seconds.
  • On a callWhile any tab uses your camera or microphone, or shares your screen, Brinky stays quiet. After a call of 30 minutes or more, it offers a break.
  • Watching or presentingVideos and fullscreen are left alone.
  • Away from your deskNothing starts on a locked screen. Three minutes away counts as rest, so you don't come back to a break.
  • In another appYou get a system notification instead of an overlay you'd never see.
  • Not right nowSnooze for 5 minutes, twice. Skipping takes a press-and-hold, so a reflex click can't throw a break away.

Install in about a minute

Brinky isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it from a folder. It's four steps, and you only do it once.

Your download has started. Here's what to do with it.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download brinky.zip and unzip it into a folder you'll keep, like Documents/Brinky. Your browser runs Brinky from there, so leave it in place.

    brinky.zip313 KB

  2. Open your extensions

    Copy this address, paste it into the address bar and press Enter. Websites aren't allowed to open it for you.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    It's the switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.

    Developer mode

  4. Load Brinky

    Click Load unpacked and choose your Brinky folder. The welcome tour opens. Pin Brinky from the puzzle-piece menu to keep it close.

    Load unpackedPack extensionUpdate

Using Edge, Brave or Arc? The steps are the same. Edge keeps Developer mode in the menu on the left.

Developer mode greyed out? Your browser is managed by a workplace or school, which blocks extensions added from a folder. Try a personal browser profile.

Questions

Is it just a 20-20-20 timer?

No. Twenty-second look-aways every twenty minutes are where it starts. Brinky also paces your water through the day, turns every third break into a few minutes of movement and nudges your posture. Work mode switches to longer focus cycles like 50/10, and SuperBrink mode lets you set every timing yourself. In every mode, it waits for calls, videos and typing.

Is Brinky really free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no premium tier.

Why do I have to install it by hand?

Brinky isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Chrome only allows one-click installs from the store, and it blocks extension files downloaded from other websites. Loading an extension from a folder is how Chrome lets you run one you've chosen to trust.

Once Brinky is in the store, installing will be one click and updates will arrive on their own.

Is it safe?

Brinky's full source code is on GitHub, so you can read every line, and the download is built from it. Brinky has no server: your browsing, your breaks and your water count never leave your computer. Only download Brinky from this page or its GitHub repository.

Why does Chrome say Brinky can "read and change all your data on all websites"?

Brinky draws the break screen and the water reminder inside the page you're looking at, and it checks whether a tab is in a call or playing a video so it knows when to wait. That needs access to every site. It doesn't read what's on the page, and it doesn't keep or send any of it.

What if I'm in the middle of something?

A small countdown appears 15 seconds before each break, so you can finish your thought or snooze for five minutes. Snoozing twice in a row is fine; the third time, the break happens. Skipping takes a deliberate press-and-hold, so a stray click can't throw a break away.

What does Brinky know about me?

Only what it needs, and only in your browser: your settings, the name you give it if you choose to, and daily counts of breaks and glasses. You can export or reset them any time. Nothing is sent anywhere. Read the privacy note.

How do I update, and how will I know there's a new version?

On GitHub, click Watch, then Custom, then Releases, and you'll get an email when a new version is out. To update, unzip the new brinky.zip into the same folder, replacing the old files, then press the reload arrow on Brinky's card in chrome://extensions. Your settings and history stay.

Chrome warns me about developer mode extensions. Is something wrong?

No. Chrome sometimes reminds you about extensions added from a folder. Keep Brinky switched on. If it ever gets turned off, switch it back on in chrome://extensions.

Which browsers and computers does it work on?

Chrome 130 or newer on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS, and Chromium browsers like Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and Vivaldi. It doesn't work in Firefox or Safari, or on phones.

Brinky doesn't show up on some pages.

Chrome doesn't let any extension draw on its own pages, like the New Tab page, chrome:// pages and the Chrome Web Store. When you're on one of those, the break opens in its own small window instead.

How do I uninstall it?

Open chrome://extensions and click Remove on Brinky's card. Then you can delete the Brinky folder.
