Why Brinky exists

Our eyes weren't made for screens.

They were made for horizons. For faces across a table, for birds overhead, for the slow change of light over a day. Not for a bright rectangle held at arm's length, ten hours at a time.

Our bodies weren't made to sit this much.

They were made to wander, to reach, to turn around and fetch water. Instead they fold toward a desk and forget to drink, until four in the afternoon, when the words start to swim.

At a screen, you blink less than half as often as you should. Your eyes dry out. The small muscle that focuses them stays clenched until evening.

None of this is a lack of willpower.

Screens are made to hold your attention, and they are very good at it. Your screen will never tell you to look away. It will never tell you to drink, or stand, or blink.

So we made a small friend who will.

A small drop of water that lives in your browser. It notices when you've been staring too long and asks, gently, at a good moment: blink. Look far away. Have a glass. Stretch a little.

It doesn't nag, and it doesn't watch. It waits while you type, stays quiet on calls, and keeps everything on your computer. It simply remembers, so you don't have to.

Be gentle with your eyes.
They have a lifetime of sunsets to see.

How it works

Three small habits, kept for you.

01 · Blink

Your cursor blinks first.

Every twenty minutes your cursor blinks a few times, so a break never arrives out of nowhere. Then Brinky takes twenty seconds with you: look far away, or follow one gentle exercise with just your eyes.

03 · Move

Your cursor moves, so you do.

Every forty-five minutes your cursor gets up for a few seconds: a long stretch, a brisk little walk, or a quick kickabout, with a small note beside it. Nothing pops up and nothing needs closing. Then it settles, and you get up too.

Questions, answered.

Is Brinky really free?

Yes. No account, no ads and no paid tier. Your settings and stats stay in your browser.

Will it interrupt my work?

It tries hard not to. A break always gets a heads-up, waits while you type, and stays away from calls and fullscreen video. One press skips or snoozes it.

Will it slow my browser down?

No. Brinky only adds itself to the tab you're looking at, at the moment it's needed. Hidden tabs pause on their own.

Why does it ask to read and change every site?

To draw a break or a glass of water on the page you're on, and to notice a call tab so it can stay quiet. It never reads what's on the page.

Can I turn water reminders off for winter?

Yes, with one switch. You can still log glasses from the toolbar and turn reminders back on any time.

How do I remove it?

On the Extensions page, press Remove on Brinky's card, then delete the folder. Nothing is left behind.
